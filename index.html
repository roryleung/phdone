🎮 How PhDone works

☀️ Today’s intention: Choose what matters today and how much focused time you realistically plan to give it.

⏱ Focus earns: productive minutes earn guilt-free spending allowance at your current focus rate.

✅ Finishing matters: completing a task adds its separate completion bonus and XP.

🔥 Weekly ranks: weekly XP resets every Monday. Rank cash rewards stay deliberately small.

🎲 The Long Game: every 25 focused minutes earns one die and completing a task earns one bonus die. The shared board now includes fictional NPC racers, tactical items, choices, micro-events, snakes, ladders, solo encounters and asynchronous duels.

🎁 Mystery boxes: personal boxes give small XP/cash surprises; board boxes mainly give tactical items. Encouragement stickers now appear on 💌 board spaces.

🔁 Habits & abstinence: reaching a habit target or abstinence milestone can still earn the reward you set.

💷 Money: PhDone remains your full record of income, essential spending, optional spending and savings.

🏛️ Lifetime milestones

Your lifetime XP never resets. It records everything you have earned through completed tasks, claimed habits and bonus XP.

Milestone bonuses are claimable once. They add spending money but do not create more XP, so they cannot trigger a chain of automatic level-ups.

The weekly rank is the main game. Lifetime levels are simply a scrapbook of sustained progress.
